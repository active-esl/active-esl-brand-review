Email signature background

One shared brand strip sits under everyone’s signature. Name, role, email and phone stay as plain text so they remain readable, searchable and copyable — even when a recipient blocks remote images. Please pick one background.

The three options

Shown on white, the way they will appear inside a message.

A · Signal Cyan tint recommended

Light and calm. Stays quiet when a thread is replied to ten times, and prints cheaply.

B · Carbon bar

Boldest brand presence. A dark block in every message, including long reply chains.

C · Carbon with cyan/violet glow

Same dark bar with the website’s hero wash and a gradient rule. Richest, and the largest file.
