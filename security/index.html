Coordinated vulnerability disclosure

Report a security issue.

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability affecting an Active-Edge product or service, please report it privately to security@active-esl.com.

Email the security team

What to include

  • The affected product or service
  • Version or build information, if known
  • The potential impact
  • Safe, concise reproduction steps
  • Your preferred contact details

Report safely

Keep sensitive information out of the first message.

Do not send passwords, cryptographic keys, personal data, customer information or unnecessary device data. If sensitive material may be necessary, describe its type without including it so that a suitable transfer route can be agreed.

Please avoid disrupting services, accessing data that is not your own, changing customer systems or publicly disclosing the issue before we have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address it.

What happens next

How reports are handled

Reports sent to this address are received by Active Edge Solutions Limited. We do not currently publish a response-time commitment. Follow-up, remediation and disclosure depend on verification, product responsibility, affected parties and applicable law.

Security support

Product-specific information

This page does not state a security support period. Product-specific support information may be added only after the applicable period, clock event and delivery route have been approved and evidenced.

Regulatory reporting

A public report is not a statutory notification.

This channel is for reporting suspected vulnerabilities to Active Edge Solutions Limited. Legal or regulatory reporting duties may use separate channels, deadlines and responsible parties. Sending a report to this address does not submit a statutory notification, and external reporters should not submit one on our behalf unless specifically authorised.
