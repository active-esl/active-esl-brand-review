Coordinated vulnerability disclosure

Report a security issue.

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability affecting an Active-Edge product or service, please report it privately to security@active-esl.com.

Email the security team

What to include

  • The affected product or service
  • Version or build information, if known
  • The potential impact
  • Safe, concise reproduction steps
  • Your preferred contact details

Report safely

Keep sensitive information out of the first message.

Do not send passwords, cryptographic keys, personal data, customer information or unnecessary device data. Tell us what sensitive material exists and we will agree a safer transfer method if it is needed.

Please avoid disrupting services, accessing data that is not your own, changing customer systems or publicly disclosing the issue before we have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address it.

What happens next

We will assess and coordinate.

We will review credible reports, seek clarification where needed and coordinate remediation and disclosure with the reporter and affected parties. Response timings will be published here once the operational process and cover have been approved and tested.

Security support

Product-specific information

Security support arrangements and periods are product- and contract-specific. Confirmed information for affected products will be published here when the product register, applicable period and delivery route have been approved.

Regulatory reporting

A public report is not a statutory notification.

This channel is for reporting vulnerabilities to Active Edge Solutions Limited. Where a legal reporting duty applies, we handle notification to the relevant authority through a restricted internal process. External reporters should not submit notifications on our behalf unless specifically authorised.
